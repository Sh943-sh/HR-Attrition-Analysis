Project - HR Attrition Analysis

project overview -
Designed and developed an interactive HR analytics report in Power BI to analyze employee attrition across 1,470 employees, uncovering an overall attrition rate.
Built dynamic visuals (donut charts, bar charts, area charts, matrix tables) to break down attrition by age, education, salary slab, gender, tenure, and job role, enabling HR stakeholders to identify high-risk employee segments.
Implemented department-level filtering (HR, R&D, Sales) using slicers/buttons for drill-down analysis across business units.
Identified key attrition drivers — e.g., employees aged 26–35 and those earning up to $5K showed the highest attrition, and Sales Executives and Laboratory Technicians had the highest attrition among job roles.
Applied DAX measures for KPIs (Attrition Rate, Avg Age, Avg Salary, Avg Years at Company) and used conditional formatting/custom visuals for an intuitive, executive-ready UI.
Delivered actionable insights that could support HR retention strategy and workforce planning decisions.


Tool/method/framework - Power BI, DAX, Data Modeling, Data Visualization, HR Analytics, KPI Design, Dashboard Design, Data Cleaning (Power Query)
